Your business does not have to do everything alone.

Synera is a platform for mutually useful collaborations between solo entrepreneurs and micro-businesses in Switzerland. We are starting in Zurich.

Use your profile to introduce your business, describe what you can contribute and explain where collaboration could help. The idea is simple: both sides should benefit.

You decide whether a connection becomes a shared idea or a concrete collaboration. Synera does not guarantee contracts, customers or business results.

STARTING LOCALLY
The first pilot focuses on Zurich. Collaborations can include meeting in person; providing a meeting location is optional.

YOUR LANGUAGE, YOUR LOOK
Synera is available in Ukrainian, German and English. Ukrainian is the default language. Choose from three looks: Current, Atelier and Night.

TEMPORARILY FREE DURING THE PILOT
The pilot is temporarily free. Any future paid offers will be explained before you choose whether to use them. Payments are not planned for the initial pilot.

Synera: create possibilities that make sense for both sides.
