THE SYNERA WAY

Designed for
both sides.

01 / CLARITY

A reason, not a rating.

See how your declared skills meet each other's needs. No score can promise chemistry or a successful business.

02 / AGENCY

Your next step. Your choice.

Review the scope, time and reward together. A suggestion is never permission to send an invitation.

03 / REALITY

Start small. Learn for real.

Choose a useful first result. Check what was delivered. Decide whether to keep working together.

FROM A NEED TO A FIRST RESULT

More than a profile.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the useful connection.

Search declared skills, needs and places. See what each person can contribute and which conditions need discussion.

02 / AGREE

Agree on the same thing.

Make the scope, time and reward clear. Each person approves the same version; changed terms need fresh approval.

03 / MEET

Choose a place. Find the way.

Browse approximate city locations. Open the agreed address and walking route in Google Maps with your own click.

04 / CHOOSE

Keep your location your choice.

Meeting-location sharing has individual consent, approximate or exact precision, a time window and revocation. Live rollout is still awaiting verification.
